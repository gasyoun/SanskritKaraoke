## Why it exists rather than another roadmap

Three roadmaps already describe *what* to build. None of them says *what is
stopping each item*, and two of the three open with a phase whose first unit is
a legal act — so a reader in phase order stops at item one. This set answers the
blocking question and leaves the product scope where it already lives.
