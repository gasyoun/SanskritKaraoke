## Gate (MG ruling D5, 21-09-2026)

A timing change ships only if (1) held-out gold mean ≤ 100 ms, (2) held-out max ≤ 250 ms on
**every** gold verse, (3) no non-gold syllable moves > 300 ms from the committed timing
without a spot-check.

## What changed

1. `tools/align_chapter.py` `detect_lead_end` takes the **longest** qualifying gap inside the
   lead window, not the first. subh_0292's lead word «subhāṣitam» has a ~150 ms closure
   before «tam»; the first-gap rule cut there and aligned «tam» as verse syllable 0.
   Regression test: [tests/test_lead_end.py](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritKaraoke/blob/main/tests/test_lead_end.py)
   (the closure test fails on the old code, ~1.62 s vs 2.49 s).
2. Gold set 5 → 10 verses, 283 high-confidence attack labels:
   [tests/fixtures/h5227_gold_attacks.json](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritKaraoke/blob/main/tests/fixtures/h5227_gold_attacks.json).
   New: subh_0513, 0605, 5909, 1221, 4693 — labels read from envelope + spectrogram plots
   **before** any assigner output on them was visible. Recension variants are labelled by
   syllable position (0605 half 2, 1221 pada 2).
3. Two assigner revisions over design 2b (stop-budget 2), in
   [docs/evidence/H5235_assigner_scripts/](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritKaraoke/tree/main/docs/evidence/H5235_assigner_scripts):
   - rev 1 `assigner5.py`: pada credit / in-pada pause penalty saturate earlier (a long
     in-word breath and a short real pada pause earn the same credit), plus an unscripted-insert
     allowance at pada crossings.
   - rev 2 `assigner6.py` (generated by `make_assigner6.py`): rev 1 + pada-crossing silence
     counted toward the pada-final syllable (`keep_sil`) + tuned prediction-pull cap.

## Failure anatomy (why max stays > 250 ms)

1. **subh_0513 (1930 ms):** an unscripted phrase 15.6–17.4 s before pada 4; the DP reads its
   onset (a 385 ms pause + 27.6 dB rise) as pada 4. Whisper had pada 4 right (17.57 vs gold 17.53).
2. **subh_0292 (1000 ms):** pada 4 «kar» at 12.04 follows a 185 ms pause; a 650 ms breath
   *inside* «kar|tavyo» at 12.39–13.04 wins the pada credit. Whisper is also wrong here (13.02).
   The two verses pull the prediction weight in opposite directions.
3. **subh_0605 (955 ms):** breath inside «mar|jitānāṃ» (510 ms) plus an irregular recension
   in half 2 with three sub-300 ms pauses near «arthāḥ».
4. Pause census over all 10 verses ([pauses.py](https://github.com/gasyoun/SanskritKaraoke/blob/main/docs/evidence/H5235_assigner_scripts/pauses.py)): pada pauses run 385–925 ms,
   in-word pauses 120–650 ms — the ranges overlap, so pause length cannot separate a pada
   boundary from a breath, and word position cannot either (Уша breathes inside words).

## What would move it (not attempted — budget spent)

1. Pada-4 anchoring needs a signal the envelope lacks: an agreement vote between whisper
   word onsets and envelope candidates per pada (0513 whisper right, 0292 whisper wrong —
   a vote only helps if a third source breaks ties), or MG/Уша session JSON corrections
   (ADR-0004's human-approved-after timings) as training anchors.
2. The gold set itself is the durable asset: any future design is scored with
   `run_eval.py <module> --loo` in about 15 min.
