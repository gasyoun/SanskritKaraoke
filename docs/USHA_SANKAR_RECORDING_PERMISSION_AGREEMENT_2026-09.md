## RECORDING USE PERMISSION AGREEMENT

**Between:** Usha Sankar (the "Licensor")
**And:** Mārcis Gasūns, for the SanskritKaraoke project (the "Licensee")
**Date:** ___ September 2026

### 1. Grant of permission

The Licensor grants the Licensee a **non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free, perpetual licence** to use, reproduce, adapt, synchronise with text and visuals, publicly perform, and distribute the Recordings (defined in §2) **as part of derivative karaoke-style educational videos** — Sanskrit verse recitations accompanied by verse text, transliteration, translation subtitles and visual overlays (the "Videos").

### 2. The Recordings

Audio recordings of Sanskrit verse recitations made by the Licensor, as listed in **Schedule A**. Schedule A may be extended by written agreement of both parties; **email is sufficient**, and each new batch of recordings supplied by the Licensor is deemed added to Schedule A unless she states otherwise.

### 3. Permitted channels

The Videos may be published on: **Telegram, Instagram (Reels), YouTube (including Shorts), TikTok**, and the SanskritKaraoke project's own website and mobile/desktop app. One vertical 9:16 master per verse may be cross-posted to these channels.

### 4. Commercial context

The Videos are **free to watch** and serve to promote paid Sanskrit courses operated by the Licensee. The Licensee shall **not** sell the Recordings as such, license or sublicense the Recordings to third parties as standalone audio, or place the Videos behind a paywall.

### 5. Attribution and metadata

Every published Video credits the Licensor, at minimum **"Recitation: Usha Sankar"**. For each verse, the project's metadata records `audio.license`, `audio.rights_holder` and `audio.permission_ref` pointing to this agreement.

### 6. Integrity

Adaptations are limited to karaoke-style derivatives: timing alignment, subtitles, captions and visuals. The Licensee will not materially distort or misrepresent the recitations and will not use them to endorse unrelated products or political content.

### 7. Takedown

The Licensor may request, by written notice, the removal of any Video not yet published or any future use; the Licensee will comply promptly. Videos already lawfully published before the notice may remain online, unless the recording is not hers to license (§8).

### 8. Rights warranty

The Licensor confirms that she holds the rights to the Recordings and is able to grant this permission. The Licensee confirms the Videos will credit her as reciter.

### 9. General

This is a friendly permissions agreement between the parties; changes require writing (email sufficient). If any clause is unenforceable, the rest stands. Governing law: the Republic of Latvia (or another jurisdiction the parties agree on in writing).
